Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun: the measure word as a Chinese character, only if you are sure of it (never romanized); otherwise nothing
After verb: nothing (irreg for an irregular verb)
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
书 -> N 本
房子 -> N
说话 -> V
小 -> Adj
