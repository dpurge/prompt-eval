Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
- Number
  sg = singular
  pl = plural (only for a plural form listed on its own)

After verb: nothing (irreg for an irregular verb)
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
مُبَرْمِجٌ -> N m sg
نِهَايَةٌ -> N f sg
اِتَّخَذَ -> V
كَانَ -> V irreg
كَبِيرٌ -> Adj
