Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
- Number: pl only for a plural form listed on its own

After verb: the group er, ir or re; irreg for an irregular verb
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
le chien -> N m
la maison -> N f
les enfants -> N m pl
parler -> V er
prendre -> V re irreg
être -> V irreg
petit -> Adj
