Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

Vietnamese has no grammatical gender and no articles.
After noun: cl and the classifier word, only if you are sure of it; otherwise nothing
After verb: nothing
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
chó -> N cl con
nhà -> N cl cái
nói chuyện -> V
nhỏ -> Adj
