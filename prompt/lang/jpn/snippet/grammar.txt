Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

Japanese has no grammatical gender and no articles: every noun is just N.
After verb: the group
  1 = godan verb (ends in a u-row consonant)
  2 = ichidan verb (ends in -iru or -eru)
  irreg = する, くる
After adjective:
  i = i-adjective
  na = na-adjective

Examples (phrase -> tags):
犬 -> N
話す -> V 1
見る -> V 2
小さい -> Adj i
きれいな -> Adj na
