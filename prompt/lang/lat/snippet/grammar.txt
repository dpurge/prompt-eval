Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

After verb: the conjugation 1, 2, 3 or 4; irreg for an irregular verb
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
rana -> N f
puer -> N m
templum -> N n
amare -> V 1
esse -> V irreg
magnus -> Adj
