The phrase is the dictionary form as the Standard Korean Language Dictionary (표준국어대사전) lists it:
- Verbs and adjectives: the stem + 다 (말하다, 작다, not 말했어요 or 작은); a 하다 verb as one word (주문하다).
- Nouns: the noun without a particle (집, not 집에서 or 집이).
- No polite or honorific endings; spacing as the dictionary has it.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
말했어요 -> 말하다
집에서 -> 집
작은 -> 작다
시작해요 -> 시작하다
