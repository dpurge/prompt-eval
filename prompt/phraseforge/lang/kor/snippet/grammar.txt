Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (명사): 집, 책
  V = Verb (동사): 말하다, 가다
  Adj = Adjective (형용사): 작다, 좋다
  Adv = Adverb (부사): 오늘, 매우
  Pron = Pronoun (대명사): 나, 그
  Conj = Conjunction (접속사): 그리고, 그러나
  Num = Numeral (수사): 하나, 셋
  Part = Particle (조사): 은/는, 이/가, 을/를
  Interj = Interjection (감탄사): 아, 와
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Examples (phrase -> tags):
집 -> N
말하다 -> V
작다 -> Adj
오늘 -> Adv
