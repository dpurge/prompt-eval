The phrase is the dictionary form as lo Zingarelli lists it:
- Nouns: the singular with the article that shows the gender: il or lo, la (il cane, lo studente, la casa); a noun starting with a vowel takes un or una instead, because l' hides the gender (un amico, un'amica); the plural only for a plural-only noun (i pantaloni).
- Verbs: the infinitive (parlare); the reflexive ending -si only for a verb that is always reflexive (pentirsi, not lavarsi).
- Adjectives: the masculine singular (piccolo, not piccola).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
i cani -> il cane
parlano -> parlare
piccole -> piccolo
gli amici -> un amico
