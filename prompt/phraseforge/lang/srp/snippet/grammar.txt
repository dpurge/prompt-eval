Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (именица): пас, жена
  V = Verb (глагол): говорити, видети
  Adj = Adjective (придев): мали, добар
  Adv = Adverb (прилог): јуче, добро
  Pron = Pronoun (заменица): ја, она
  Prep = Preposition (предлог): у, са
  Conj = Conjunction (везник): и, али
  Num = Numeral (број): два, три
  Part = Particle (речца): не, ли
  Interj = Interjection (узвик): ах, ох
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Aspect, after a verb (always lower case):
  impf = imperfective
  pf = perfective

Examples (phrase -> tags):
пас -> N m
жена -> N f
село -> N n
говорити -> V impf
дати -> V pf
мали -> Adj
