The phrase is the dictionary form as the Rečnik srpskoga jezika (Matica srpska) lists it:
- Nouns: the nominative singular (пас, жена, село), not an oblique case or the plural.
- Verbs: the infinitive (говорити); се only for a verb that is always reflexive (смејати се). The two aspects are separate headwords.
- Adjectives: the masculine nominative singular, indefinite (мали, not мала).
- Script as the lesson writes it (Cyrillic).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
пса -> пас
говорио -> говорити
мала -> мали
смејао се -> смејати се
