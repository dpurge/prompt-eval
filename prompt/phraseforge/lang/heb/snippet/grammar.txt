Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine

After verb: the verb pattern, lower case (qal, piel, hifil, ...); irreg for an irregular verb
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
כלב -> N m
אישה -> N f
לדבר -> V piel
לראות -> V qal
קטן -> Adj
