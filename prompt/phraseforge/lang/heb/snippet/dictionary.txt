The phrase is the dictionary form as the Even-Shoshan dictionary lists it:
- Nouns: the singular as the dictionary lists it (כלב, אישה), not the plural or the construct form unless it is the headword (עיניים).
- Verbs: the infinitive with ל (לכתוב), not the past, present or future form.
- Adjectives: the masculine singular (גדול, not גדולה).
- No attached prefixes (ה, ו, ב, ל, מ, ש, כ): not והכלב. Write the niqqud only if the lesson text has it.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
הכלבים -> כלב
כתבתי -> לכתוב
גדולה -> גדול
ובבית -> בית
