Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

Finnish has no grammatical gender and no articles: every noun is just N.
After verb: the type 1 to 6; irreg for an irregular verb
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
koira -> N
puhua -> V 1
nähdä -> V 4 irreg
olla -> V irreg
pieni -> Adj
