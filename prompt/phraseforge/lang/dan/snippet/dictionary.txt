The phrase is the dictionary form as Den Danske Ordbog lists it:
- Nouns: the indefinite singular with en or et, which shows the gender (en hund, et hus), not hunden, hundene or hunde.
- Verbs: the infinitive without at (tale, not at tale or taler).
- Adjectives: the base form (lille, not små; stor, not store).
- No article for a name. Spelling: æ, ø, å kept.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
hundene -> en hund
huset -> et hus
talte -> tale
store -> stor
