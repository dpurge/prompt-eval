Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (navneord): hund, hus
  V = Verb (udsagnsord): tale, være
  Adj = Adjective (tillægsord): lille, god
  Adv = Adverb (biord): i går, godt
  Pron = Pronoun (stedord): jeg, hun
  Prep = Preposition (forholdsord): i, med
  Conj = Conjunction (bindeord): og, men
  Num = Numeral (talord): to, tre
  Part = Particle (partikel): ikke, jo
  Interj = Interjection (udråbsord): av, åh
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  c = common (en)
  n = neuter (et)

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
en hund -> N c
et hus -> N n
tale -> V
lille -> Adj
