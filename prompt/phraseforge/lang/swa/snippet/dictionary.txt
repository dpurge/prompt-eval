The phrase is the dictionary form as the Kamusi ya Kiswahili Sanifu lists it:
- Nouns: the singular with its prefix (mtoto, kitabu), not the plural (watoto, vitabu).
- Verbs: the infinitive with ku- (kusema, kuona), not a form with a subject or tense prefix (alisema, anaona).
- Adjectives: the form with the class 1 prefix (mkubwa, mzuri), not the form of another noun class (kikubwa).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
watoto -> mtoto
alisema -> kusema
kikubwa -> mkubwa
vitabu -> kitabu
