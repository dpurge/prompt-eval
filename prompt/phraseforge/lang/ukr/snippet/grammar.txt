Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter
- an for an animate masculine noun

After verb:
  impf = imperfective
  pf = perfective
  irreg = irregular
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
собака -> N f
будинок -> N m
говорити -> V impf
побачити -> V pf
маленький -> Adj
