The phrase is the dictionary form as the O'zbek tilining izohli lug'ati lists it:
- Nouns: the bare singular (uy, not uylarimizda).
- Verbs: the infinitive in -moq (gapirmoq, not gapirdi).
- Adjectives: the base form (katta, not kattaroq).
- Spelling: o', g', sh, ch, ng as in the lesson.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
uylarimizda -> uy
gapirdi -> gapirmoq
kattaroq -> katta
kitoblar -> kitob
