The phrase is the dictionary form as Van Dale lists it:
- Nouns: the singular with de or het, which shows the gender (de hond, het huis); the plural only for a plural-only noun (de ouders).
- Verbs: the infinitive with a separable prefix attached (opstaan, not staat ... op); zich only for a verb that is always reflexive (zich vergissen).
- Adjectives: the base form without -e (klein, not kleine).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
de honden -> de hond
stond ... op -> opstaan
kleine -> klein
vergiste zich -> zich vergissen
