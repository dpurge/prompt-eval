Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun: the article of the word
  de = common gender
  het = neuter
- Number: pl only for a plural form listed on its own

After verb:
  sep = separable prefix
  irreg = irregular
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
de hond -> N de
het huis -> N het
praten -> V
opstaan -> V sep
zien -> V irreg
klein -> Adj
