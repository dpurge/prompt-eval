Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (名詞): 犬, 本
  V = Verb (動詞): 話す, 見る
  Adj = Adjective (形容詞, 形容動詞): 小さい, きれい
  Adv = Adverb (副詞): とても, もう
  Pron = Pronoun (代名詞): 私, これ
  Conj = Conjunction (接続詞): そして, しかし
  Num = Numeral (数詞): 一, 三
  Part = Particle (助詞): は, が, を
  Interj = Interjection (感動詞): ああ, はい
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Class, after an adjective (always lower case):
  i = i-adjective (ends in -い)
  na = na-adjective (the form without な)

Examples (phrase -> tags):
犬 -> N
話す -> V
小さい -> Adj i
きれい -> Adj na
は -> Part
