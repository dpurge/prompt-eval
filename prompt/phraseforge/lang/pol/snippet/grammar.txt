Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (rzeczownik): pies, kobieta
  V = Verb (czasownik): mówić, widzieć
  Adj = Adjective (przymiotnik): mały, dobry
  Adv = Adverb (przysłówek): wczoraj, dobrze
  Pron = Pronoun (zaimek): ja, ona
  Prep = Preposition (przyimek): w, z
  Conj = Conjunction (spójnik): i, ale
  Num = Numeral (liczebnik): dwa, trzy
  Part = Particle (partykuła): nie, już
  Interj = Interjection (wykrzyknik): ach, oj
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Aspect, after a verb (always lower case):
  impf = imperfective
  pf = perfective

Examples (phrase -> tags):
pies -> N m
kobieta -> N f
dziecko -> N n
mówić -> V impf
zobaczyć -> V pf
mały -> Adj
