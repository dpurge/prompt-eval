Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (съществително име): куче, къща
  V = Verb (глагол): говоря, видя
  Adj = Adjective (прилагателно име): голям, добър
  Adv = Adverb (наречие): вчера, добре
  Pron = Pronoun (местоимение): аз, тя
  Prep = Preposition (предлог): в, с
  Conj = Conjunction (съюз): и, но
  Num = Numeral (числително име): два, три
  Part = Particle (частица): не, ли
  Interj = Interjection (междуметие): ах, ох
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Aspect, after a verb (always lower case):
  impf = imperfective
  pf = perfective

Examples (phrase -> tags):
куче -> N n
къща -> N f
говоря -> V impf
видя -> V pf
голям -> Adj
