The phrase is the dictionary form as Речник на българския език (RBE) lists it:
- Nouns: the singular without the article (куче, not кучето or кучета).
- Verbs: the 1st person singular present (говоря, видя): Bulgarian has no infinitive. The two aspects are separate headwords; се only for a verb that is always reflexive (смея се).
- Adjectives: the masculine singular without the article (голям, not голямата).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
кучетата -> куче
говорим -> говоря
голямата -> голям
видях -> видя
