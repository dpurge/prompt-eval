Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (nomen): rana, puer
  V = Verb (verbum): amo, sum
  Adj = Adjective (adiectivum): magnus, parvus
  Adv = Adverb (adverbium): heri, bene
  Pron = Pronoun (pronomen): ego, ille
  Prep = Preposition (praepositio): in, cum
  Conj = Conjunction (coniunctio): et, sed
  Num = Numeral (numerale): duo, tres
  Part = Particle (particula): non, -ne
  Interj = Interjection (interiectio): heu, o
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
rana -> N f
puer -> N m
templum -> N n
amo -> V
magnus -> Adj
