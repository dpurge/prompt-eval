The phrase is the dictionary form as Lewis and Short lists it:
- Nouns: the nominative singular (rana, puer, templum), not an oblique case or the plural.
- Verbs: the 1st person singular present active indicative (amo, vitupero), not the infinitive or the perfect; a deponent verb in its passive form (loquor).
- Adjectives: the masculine nominative singular (magnus, parvus).
- Macrons are not required.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
ranae -> rana
amant -> amo
parvam -> parvus
pueri -> puer
