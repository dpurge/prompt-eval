Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (संज्ञा): लड़का, घर
  V = Verb (क्रिया): करना, जाना
  Adj = Adjective (विशेषण): बड़ा, अच्छा
  Adv = Adverb (क्रियाविशेषण): आज, यहाँ
  Pron = Pronoun (सर्वनाम): मैं, वह
  Post = Postposition (संबंधबोधक): में, पर, का
  Conj = Conjunction (समुच्चयबोधक): और, लेकिन
  Num = Numeral (संख्यावाचक): एक, तीन
  Part = Particle (निपात): ही, भी
  Interj = Interjection (विस्मयादिबोधक): अरे, वाह
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
लड़का -> N m
लड़की -> N f
करना -> V
बड़ा -> Adj
में -> Post
