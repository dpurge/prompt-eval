The phrase is the dictionary headword as a Mandarin dictionary lists it:
- Chinese has no inflection: never add particles or aspect markers to a verb (吃, not 吃了 or 吃过) and no plural 们 on a noun (学生, not 学生们); 我们, 你们 and 他们 are headwords of their own.
- Prefer the word (词), usually 1-4 characters, not a whole clause; a verb-object compound listed in dictionaries is kept together (说话, 吃饭).
- Reduplicated or resultative forms are given as the base word (看, not 看看; 看见 only when it is a headword).
- Use the script of the lesson (Simplified or Traditional) consistently and do not mix them; no punctuation, no measure word attached to the noun.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
吃了 -> 吃
学生们 -> 学生
看看 -> 看
一本书 -> 书
