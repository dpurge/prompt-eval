The phrase is the dictionary form as the Oxford English Dictionary lists it:
- Nouns: the singular without an article (dog, not dogs or a dog; child, not children).
- Verbs: the base form without to (speak, not speaks, spoke or to speak); a phrasal verb with its particle (give up, look after).
- Adjectives and adverbs: the base form (small, not smaller; quickly).
- Lower case, except for names.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
dogs -> dog
gave up -> give up
smaller -> small
children -> child
