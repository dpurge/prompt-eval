Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun: dog, house
  V = Verb: speak, give up
  Adj = Adjective: small, good
  Adv = Adverb: yesterday, well
  Pron = Pronoun: I, she
  Prep = Preposition: in, with
  Conj = Conjunction: and, but
  Num = Numeral: two, three
  Part = Particle: not, to
  Interj = Interjection: oh, ouch
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Examples (phrase -> tags):
dog -> N
give up -> V
small -> Adj
quickly -> Adv
