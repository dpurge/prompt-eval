The phrase is the dictionary form as the UP Diksiyonaryong Filipino lists it:
- Nouns: the root word without ang, ng, sa or mga (bahay, not ang bahay or mga bahay).
- Verbs: the infinitive with its affix (magsalita, kumain), not a conjugated form (nagsasalita, kumakain).
- Adjectives: the base form (maliit, not napakaliit).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
mga bahay -> bahay
nagsasalita -> magsalita
kumakain -> kumain
napakaliit -> maliit
