The phrase is the dictionary form as Slovník spisovného jazyka českého lists it:
- Nouns: the nominative singular (pes, not psa or psi); the plural only for a plural-only noun (dveře).
- Verbs: the infinitive (mluvit); se or si only for a verb that is always reflexive (smát se). The two aspects are separate headwords.
- Adjectives: the nominative singular masculine (malý, not malé).
- Spelling: háčky and čárky kept (ř, š, č, ž, ě, ů).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
psa -> pes
mluvila -> mluvit
malé -> malý
smál se -> smát se
