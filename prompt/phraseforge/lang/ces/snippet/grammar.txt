Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (podstatné jméno): pes, žena
  V = Verb (sloveso): mluvit, vidět
  Adj = Adjective (přídavné jméno): malý, dobrý
  Adv = Adverb (příslovce): včera, dobře
  Pron = Pronoun (zájmeno): já, ona
  Prep = Preposition (předložka): v, s
  Conj = Conjunction (spojka): a, ale
  Num = Numeral (číslovka): dva, tři
  Part = Particle (částice): ne, až
  Interj = Interjection (citoslovce): ach, fuj
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Aspect, after a verb (always lower case):
  impf = imperfective
  pf = perfective

Examples (phrase -> tags):
pes -> N m
žena -> N f
město -> N n
mluvit -> V impf
uvidět -> V pf
malý -> Adj
