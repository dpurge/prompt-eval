Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (nom): le chien, la maison
  V = Verb (verbe): parler, finir
  Adj = Adjective (adjectif): grand, petit
  Adv = Adverb (adverbe): hier, bien
  Pron = Pronoun (pronom): je, elle
  Prep = Preposition (préposition): dans, avec
  Conj = Conjunction (conjonction): et, mais
  Num = Numeral (numéral): deux, trois
  Part = Particle (particule): ne, pas
  Interj = Interjection (interjection): ah, aïe
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
le chien -> N m
la maison -> N f
les gens -> N m pl
parler -> V
petit -> Adj
