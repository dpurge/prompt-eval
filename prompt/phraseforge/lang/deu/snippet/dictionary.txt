The phrase is the dictionary form as the Duden lists it:
- Nouns: nominative singular with the definite article: der Hund, die Frau, das Kind. A plural form only for a plural-only noun (die Eltern, die Leute). No article in the phrase for a name.
- Verbs: the infinitive, with the separable prefix attached (aufstehen, not stehen ... auf); reflexive verbs with sich (sich freuen).
- Adjectives and participles used as adjectives: the uninflected base form (klein, not kleinen); a participle only when it is a headword of its own (geschlossen).
- Spelling: current German orthography; ß, ä, ö, ü kept; nouns capitalized, everything else lower case.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
den Kindern -> das Kind
ging ... aus -> ausgehen
freut sich -> sich freuen
kleinen -> klein
