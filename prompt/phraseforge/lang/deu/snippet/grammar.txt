Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter
- Number: pl only for a plural form listed on its own

After verb:
  sep = separable prefix
  refl = reflexive
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
der Hund -> N m
die Bücher -> N n pl
aufstehen -> V sep
sich freuen -> V refl
klein -> Adj
