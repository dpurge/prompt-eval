Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter
- Declension: 1, 2 or 3; irreg for an irregular noun

After verb: contract for a contract verb; irreg for an irregular verb
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
ὁ κύων, κυνός -> N m 3
ἡ οἰκία, -ας -> N f 1
τὸ παιδίον, -ου -> N n 2
λέγω -> V
ὁράω -> V contract
μικρός -> Adj
