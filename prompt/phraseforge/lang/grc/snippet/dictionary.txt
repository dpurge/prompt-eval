The phrase is the dictionary form as the Liddell-Scott-Jones lexicon (LSJ) lists it:
- Nouns: the nominative singular with the article that shows the gender (ὁ λόγος, ἡ οἰκία, τὸ παιδίον); no article for a name.
- Verbs: the 1st person singular present active indicative (λύω, λέγω); a deponent verb in its middle or passive form (ἔρχομαι). Not the infinitive, the aorist or another tense.
- Adjectives: the masculine nominative singular (ἀγαθός, not ἀγαθή).
- Accents and breathings kept; no elision or crasis (not δ' for δέ).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
τοῦ λόγου -> ὁ λόγος
ἔλυσε -> λύω
ἀγαθῆς -> ἀγαθός
λέγειν -> λέγω
