Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine
  f = feminine
  n = neuter

After verb: the type A (-ω) or B (-ώ, -άω); irreg for an irregular verb
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
ο σκύλος -> N m
το σπίτι -> N n
η γυναίκα -> N f
βλέπω -> V A
μιλώ -> V B
μικρός -> Adj
