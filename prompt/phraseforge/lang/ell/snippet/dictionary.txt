The phrase is the dictionary form as the Dictionary of Standard Modern Greek (Triantafyllidis) lists it:
- Nouns: the nominative singular with the article that shows the gender: ο, η or το (ο σκύλος, η γυναίκα, το παιδί); no article for a name.
- Verbs: the 1st person singular present (μιλώ, βλέπω): Greek has no infinitive. Not the aorist, the future or the passive form unless it is the only form (έρχομαι).
- Adjectives: the masculine nominative singular (μικρός, not μικρή).
- Monotonic spelling with one accent mark; no elision (not μ' αγαπά).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
των παιδιών -> το παιδί
μιλάει -> μιλώ
μικρή -> μικρός
είδα -> βλέπω
