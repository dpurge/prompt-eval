Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun: דער הונט, די פֿרוי
  V = Verb: רעדן, זײַן
  Adj = Adjective: קליין, גוט
  Adv = Adverb: נעכטן, גוט
  Pron = Pronoun: איך, זי
  Prep = Preposition: אין, מיט
  Conj = Conjunction: און, אָבער
  Num = Numeral: צוויי, דרײַ
  Part = Particle: נישט, דען
  Interj = Interjection: אוי, ווײַ
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine (דער)
  f = feminine (די)
  n = neuter (דאָס)

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
דער הונט -> N m
די פֿרוי -> N f
דאָס הויז -> N n
רעדן -> V
קליין -> Adj
