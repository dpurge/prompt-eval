Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags:

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = noun
  V = verb
  Adj = adjective
  Adv = adverb
  Pron = pronoun
  Prep = preposition
  Conj = conjunction
  Num = numeral
  Part = particle
  Phrase = fixed phrase or idiom

After noun:
- Gender (always lower case)
  m = masculine (דער)
  f = feminine (די)
  n = neuter (דאָס)

After verb: nothing (irreg for an irregular verb)
After adjective: nothing

Examples (phrase -> tags):
דער הונט -> N m
דאָס הויז -> N n
די פרוי -> N f
רעדן -> V
זיין -> V irreg
קליין -> Adj
