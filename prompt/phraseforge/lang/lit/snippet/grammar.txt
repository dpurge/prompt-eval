Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (daiktavardis): šuo, moteris
  V = Verb (veiksmažodis): kalbėti, matyti
  Adj = Adjective (būdvardis): didelis, geras
  Adv = Adverb (prieveiksmis): vakar, gerai
  Pron = Pronoun (įvardis): aš, ji
  Prep = Preposition (prielinksnis): į, su
  Conj = Conjunction (jungtukas): ir, bet
  Num = Numeral (skaitvardis): du, trys
  Part = Particle (dalelytė): ne, gi
  Interj = Interjection (jaustukas): ak, oi
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
šuo -> N m
moteris -> N f
kalbėti -> V
didelis -> Adj
