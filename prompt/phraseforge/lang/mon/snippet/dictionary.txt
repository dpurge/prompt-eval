The phrase is the dictionary form as the Mongolian explanatory dictionary lists it:
- Nouns: the nominative singular without case, plural or possessive endings (гэр, not гэрт or гэрүүд).
- Verbs: the form in -х (бичих), not бичсэн or бичнэ.
- Adjectives: the base form (том).
- Cyrillic as the lesson writes it, with ө and ү.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
гэрүүдэд -> гэр
бичсэн -> бичих
хүүхдүүд -> хүүхэд
томоор -> том
