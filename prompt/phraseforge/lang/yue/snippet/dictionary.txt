The phrase is the dictionary form as the Cantonese dictionary (粵典, words.hk) lists it:
- No aspect marker, plural or measure word attached (食, not 食咗; 學生, not 學生哋; 書, not 一本書).
- Keep the Cantonese word (冇, 嘅, 咗, 佢) and do not replace it with the Standard Chinese one.
- A verb-object compound stays together (食飯); a word is usually 1-4 characters.
- Use the script of the lesson (Traditional or Simplified) consistently.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
食咗 -> 食
學生哋 -> 學生
一本書 -> 書
食緊飯 -> 食飯
