Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (名詞): 書, 人
  V = Verb (動詞): 食, 去
  Adj = Adjective (形容詞): 大, 好
  Adv = Adverb (副詞): 好, 都
  Pron = Pronoun (代詞): 我, 佢
  Prep = Preposition (介詞): 喺, 同
  Conj = Conjunction (連詞): 同, 但係
  Num = Numeral (數詞): 一, 三
  Part = Particle (助詞): 嘅, 咗, 嗎
  Interj = Interjection (嘆詞): 哎, 嘩
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

After noun: the measure word as a Chinese character, only if you are sure of it (never romanized)

Examples (phrase -> tags):
書 -> N 本
狗 -> N 隻
食 -> V
大 -> Adj
