The phrase is the dictionary form as a Levantine Arabic dictionary lists it:
- Nouns and adjectives: the singular without the article (كتاب, not الكتاب); an adjective in the masculine singular (كبير, not كبيرة). A broken plural only when it is a separate headword.
- Verbs: the 3rd person masculine singular perfect (كتب), never a form with بـ, عم, رح or a pronoun ending.
- Keep the colloquial word as the lesson spells it; do not replace it with the Standard Arabic one (بدي, not أريد).
- No attached prepositions, conjunctions or pronoun suffixes (not وكتابه).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
البنات -> بنت
بيكتبوا -> كتب
كتابه -> كتاب
كبيرة -> كبير
