Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (اِسْم): كِتَاب، بَيْت
  V = Verb (فِعْل): كَتَب، راح
  Adj = Adjective (صِفَة): كْبِير، حِلْو
  Adv = Adverb (ظَرْف): هَلَّق، هُون
  Pron = Pronoun (ضَمِير): أَنَا، هُوِّي
  Prep = Preposition (حَرْف جَرّ): مِن، عَلَى
  Conj = Conjunction (حَرْف عَطْف): وَ، بَسّ
  Num = Numeral (عَدَد): تَلَاتَة، عَشَرَة
  Part = Particle (أَدَاة): مَا، عَم
  Interj = Interjection: آخ، يَا سَلَام
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  m = masculine
  f = feminine

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
كِتَاب -> N m
مَدْرَسَة -> N f
كَتَب -> V
كْبِير -> Adj
