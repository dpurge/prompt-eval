The phrase is the dictionary form as Kamus Besar Bahasa Indonesia (KBBI) lists it:
- Nouns: the singular, without reduplication or a suffix (anjing, not anjing-anjing; rumah, not rumahnya).
- Verbs: the base word (baca, tulis), not the form with me-, ber-, di-, -kan or -i, unless the derived word has a meaning of its own (belajar, mengajar).
- Adjectives: the base form (kecil, not terkecil or kekecilan).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
membaca -> baca
anjing-anjing -> anjing
ditulis -> tulis
rumahnya -> rumah
