Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (kata benda): rumah, anjing
  V = Verb (kata kerja): berbicara, melihat
  Adj = Adjective (kata sifat): kecil, baik
  Adv = Adverb (kata keterangan): kemarin, sangat
  Pron = Pronoun (kata ganti): saya, dia
  Prep = Preposition (kata depan): di, ke, dari
  Conj = Conjunction (kata sambung): dan, tetapi
  Num = Numeral (kata bilangan): dua, tiga
  Part = Particle (partikel): lah, kah
  Interj = Interjection (kata seru): wah, aduh
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Examples (phrase -> tags):
rumah -> N
berbicara -> V
kecil -> Adj
di -> Prep
