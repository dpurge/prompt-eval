The phrase is the dictionary form as an Arabic dictionary (Hans Wehr) lists it, fully vocalized:
- Nouns and adjectives: the indefinite nominative singular with tanwīn, without the article: كَلْبٌ, not الكَلْب. A broken plural only when it is a separate headword.
- Adjectives: masculine singular (كَبِيرٌ, not كَبِيرَةٌ).
- Verbs: 3rd person masculine singular perfect of form I-X (كَتَبَ, اِسْتَخْدَمَ), never the imperfect or the masdar.
- Keep the short vowels, shadda and hamza as the dictionary prints them (سُؤَالٌ, not سوال); tāʾ marbūṭa for feminine nouns (مَدْرَسَةٌ).
- No attached prepositions, conjunctions or pronoun suffixes (not وَكِتَابُهُ).

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
الطُّلَّابُ -> طَالِبٌ
يَكْتُبُونَ -> كَتَبَ
كِتَابُهُ -> كِتَابٌ
الْمَدْرَسَةِ -> مَدْرَسَةٌ
