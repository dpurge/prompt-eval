Grammar tags is an ordered string of space-separated tags, written exactly as listed (tags are case sensitive):

Part of speech (always capitalized):
  N = Noun (substantiv): en hund, ett hus
  V = Verb (verb): tala, vara
  Adj = Adjective (adjektiv): liten, bra
  Adv = Adverb (adverb): igår, bra
  Pron = Pronoun (pronomen): jag, hon
  Prep = Preposition (preposition): i, med
  Conj = Conjunction (konjunktion): och, men
  Num = Numeral (räkneord): två, tre
  Part = Particle (partikel): inte, ju
  Interj = Interjection (interjektion): aj, åh
  Ph = Fixed phrase or idiom

Gender, after a noun (always lower case):
  c = common (en)
  n = neuter (ett)

Number, after a noun, only for a plural form listed on its own (always lower case):
  pl = plural

Examples (phrase -> tags):
en hund -> N c
ett hus -> N n
tala -> V
liten -> Adj
