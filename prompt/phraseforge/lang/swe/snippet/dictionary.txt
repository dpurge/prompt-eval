The phrase is the dictionary form as Svenska Akademiens ordlista (SAOL) lists it:
- Nouns: the indefinite singular with en or ett, which shows the gender (en hund, ett hus), not hunden, hundar or hundarna.
- Verbs: the infinitive without att (tala, not att tala or talar).
- Adjectives: the common-gender base form (liten, not litet or små).
- Spelling: å ä ö kept.

Examples (found in text -> phrase):
hundarna -> en hund
huset -> ett hus
talade -> tala
små -> liten
